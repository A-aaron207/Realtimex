   ## What you've done well                                                                                
                                                                                                           
   * **Clear feature freeze.** Prevents scope creep.                                                       
   * **Objective KPIs.** You're tracking reliability instead of feelings.                                  
   * **Severity levels.** P0/P1/P2 help prioritize work.

   * **Performance budgets.** Great addition—performance is part of correctness.

   What I would prioritize next                                                                            
   Planner reliability (98%+ capability selection)                                                         
   Memory reliability (variables, task outputs, workspace context)                                         
   Action Validator before execution                                                                       
   Response synthesizer that converts raw tool output into user-friendly responses                         
   End-to-end scenario tests (developer, researcher, sysadmin, casual user)                                
   Boot-time optimization (reduce from ~860 ms toward your <500 ms target)                                 
                                                                                                           
   Once those are solid, then I'd consider larger features such as voice interaction, autonomous           
   background agents, or more sophisticated multi-model orchestration. At that point, Realtimex would have 
   a dependable foundation rather than accumulating technical debt through additional capabilities.
